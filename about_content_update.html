Our Vision & Mission

Jal Rakshak represents a paradigm shift in water conservation methodology. Our team of dedicated students from St. Joseph Sr. Sec. School, Bhopal, has undertaken the responsibility of developing innovative grey water treatment solutions that can be implemented at both household and community levels. We believe that sustainable water management is not just a necessity but a moral obligation to future generations.
